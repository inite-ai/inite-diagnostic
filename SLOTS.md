## What it asks about

The free eight:

- `industry` — what the business actually does, and for whom
- `channels` — where inbound arrives — WhatsApp, email, Instagram, the phone, a form
- `crm` — where customer data is kept today, including "a spreadsheet" and "nowhere"
- `dailyInquiries` — how many inquiries arrive on an ordinary day, as a range
- `dailyLeads` — how many of those turn into qualified leads, as a range
- `teamSize` — how many people spend a meaningful part of their day on this work
- `painPoint` — where the process breaks, or what it costs them when it does
- `goal` — what they want to be true in ninety days

And, at the premium depth, thirty-five more:


**Strategy**

- `businessModel` — how money is actually made — subscription, project, retainer, resale, commission
- `growthTarget` — the number they want to move and by when, in their own terms
- `marketPosition` — who they win against today and why the customer picks them
- `competitors` — who else the buyer considers, named if they will name them
- `expansionPlans` — a new market, product or segment already decided on rather than wished for
- `decisionMaker` — who signs off on a change to this process, and who can veto it

**Marketing**

- `acquisitionChannels` — where customers come from, ranked by volume rather than by hope
- `salesCycle` — how long from first contact to signature, as a typical range
- `conversionRate` — what share of leads become customers, even as a rough fraction
- `firstResponseTime` — how long an inbound message waits before a human answers it
- `followUpDiscipline` — what happens to a lead that does not reply — chased, queued, or lost
- `marketingTools` — what they already pay for that touches marketing or sales
- `averageDealSize` — what one customer is worth on signature, as a range

**Operations**

- `workflowSteps` — the hand-offs an order or enquiry passes through, in order
- `timeConsumingTasks` — the work that eats the most hours and produces the least judgement
- `bottleneck` — the one step everything queues behind when volume rises
- `manualHours` — hours a week the team spends on work a machine could do, as their own estimate
- `errorRate` — how often the process goes wrong, and what it costs when it does
- `scalingConstraint` — what breaks first if the volume doubles tomorrow
- `documentation` — whether the process is written down anywhere or lives in somebody
- `seasonality` — whether load is even through the year or arrives in waves

**Finance**

- `revenueRange` — annual revenue as a band — the answer sizes every recommendation after it
- `revenueStreams` — where the money comes from, and which stream is growing
- `costStructure` — the largest costs, and which of them scale with volume
- `pricingModel` — how they price, and when the price last changed
- `marginPressure` — whether margin is holding, and what is squeezing it
- `budgetCapacity` — what they can commit to a change without a new approval round
- `paybackExpectation` — how fast an investment has to return before it stops being worth doing

**Tech**

- `techStack` — the systems the work runs through today, including spreadsheets
- `dataLocation` — where the data that matters lives, and whether one system can read another
- `integrations` — what is already wired to what, and what is copied across by hand
- `automationLevel` — what already runs without a person, however small
- `aiUsage` — what they have already tried with AI, and how it went
- `technicalCapacity` — whether anyone inside can build or maintain, or every change is bought
- `complianceConstraints` — rules about where data may go — jurisdiction, sector, a customer contract
